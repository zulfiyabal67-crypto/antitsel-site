The Anti-Goal Method

Sometimes the difficulty is not a lack of willpower. The harder we try to force ourselves, the more tension, fatigue and a sense of being stuck may grow.

The Anti-Goal Method invites you to notice a recurring pattern — an “inner loop” — and explore what you no longer want to maintain. From there, you can look for a small action that feels possible now.

The aim is not to give up on goals or dreams. It is to make room for a more honest understanding of yourself and your next step.
